# Learning Protocol

Use this whenever the team or the creative lead gives feedback, a cut succeeds, a cut fails, or an export reveals a reusable rule.

## Learning Trigger

Treat these as learning triggers:

- direct correction from the team or the creative lead
- repeated edit failure
- pacing that feels wrong
- captions too large, too small, too busy, or unsafe for platform UI
- audio pop, bad fade, mismatched ambience, or music overpowering image
- final cut loses mood, story, or product clarity
- a cutdown works better than the master
- an export setting becomes reusable

## Required Action

Do not rely on silent memory. Update one or more of:

- the active skill
- a template
- a retrieval map
- a project note
- the named client's feedback log, pattern ledger, or style profile
- `16 Learning and Creative Cycles/Agent Learning Protocol.md`
- the relevant output log

## Client Feedback Learning

When feedback belongs to a named client, first update `22 Local Style Memory/Clients/<Client Name>/Feedback Log.md`.

- One occurrence stays a project note.
- Two similar occurrences become a candidate pattern in the client's `Pattern Ledger.md`.
- Three similar occurrences or one explicit standing instruction become a stable rule in the client's `Client Style Profile.md`.
- Client-specific edit preferences should become final-cut constraints for that client, not global edit rules, unless the team or the creative lead explicitly generalizes them.
